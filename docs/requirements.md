# Requirements (high level)

The **Developer Incident Management Platform** is intended for engineering organizations that need a single place to coordinate operational work during and after incidents.

## Product scope (intended)

The platform is expected to support, over time:

- Incident lifecycle management
- Service catalog and ownership
- Teams and organizational structure
- On-call schedules
- Escalation policies
- Notifications
- Postmortems
- Deployments and change correlation
- Audit logs
- External monitoring and alerting integrations

## Out of scope for foundation phase

This repository phase establishes tooling, architecture, and conventions only. Detailed business rules, domain entities, and user workflows are intentionally deferred to later phases.
